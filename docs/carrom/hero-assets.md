# Hero Assets

The Hero Asset system designates specific crucial meshes (Board, Striker, Queen, Coins, Pockets) as priority items.
These receive:
- Max resolution textures
- Premium shaders (Clearcoat/Transmission)
- Complex physics colliders (Cylinders and Boxes for stability)
- Specialized particle interactions

Non-hero assets (background furniture, room details) are downgraded to simple Lambert/Phong materials to preserve bandwidth.
